# Social Engineering

## Objective
Understand social engineering techniques through a safe phishing-awareness simulation in a controlled environment.

## Simulation
A phishing simulation was studied to understand how attackers may use deceptive login pages or messages to trick users into revealing information.

## What I Learned
- Understanding phishing attacks
- Identifying suspicious login pages and links
- Recognizing social engineering indicators
- Understanding how user awareness helps prevent phishing
- Importance of verifying URLs before entering credentials

## Safety
This exercise is an awareness simulation only. No real credentials or personal information were collected.

## Mitigation
- Verify URLs before clicking
- Avoid entering credentials on suspicious pages
- Use multi-factor authentication
- Report suspicious messages
- Conduct regular security-awareness training

## Evidence
Screenshots of the awareness simulation will be added as proof of concept.

## Key Takeaway
Human awareness is an important layer of cybersecurity. Recognizing phishing indicators can significantly reduce the risk of credential theft.
